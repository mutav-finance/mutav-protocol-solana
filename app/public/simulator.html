    How this is computed

    Monthly steps m = 1..N. The reserve is a balance simulated month by month and checked against the required reserve. The solvency rule follows the reserve program (spec §4): only the surplus above the required reserve can back new guarantees or leave.

    // capital
capital_in(m)        = starting_capital                     if m = 1
                     + monthly_raise                        if from ≤ m ≤ until (until 0 = N)

// required reserve per lease
coverage policy      = max(ceiling_share × cover_per_lease, max(1, bad_years) × tail_per_lease)
                       cover_per_lease = rent × (arrears_ceiling + exit_ceiling)
                       tail_per_lease  = expected_claims × bad_year_multiplier
compare: full (c)    = c × cover_per_lease
compare: exp.-loss   = tail_years × tail_per_lease
required(m)          = active(m) × required_per_lease

// solvency gate (register_guarantee is gated on free capital)
opening(m)           = balance(m−1) + capital_in(m)
new(m)               = min(new_per_month, floor((opening(m) − existing(m) × required_per_lease) / required_per_lease))
                       blocked when new(m) < new_per_month; the book holds, claims are still paid
active(m)            = leases signed in the last lease_term months

// flows
guarantee_fees(m)    = active(m) × rent × fee × fee_periods_per_year / 12
mutav_take(m)        = guarantee_fees(m) × take                 // treasury, never in the reserve
net_fees(m)          = guarantee_fees(m) − mutav_take(m)
claims_paid(m)       = active(m) × expected_claims / 12 × ramp(m)         ramp(m) = min(1, m/4) if on
                     + active(m) × (tail_per_lease − expected_claims) / 12   in the 12 months from the bad-year month
yield(m)             = opening(m) × tesouro_share × tesouro_rate / 12
net_gain(m)          = net_fees(m) + yield(m) − claims_paid(m)

balance(m)           = balance(m−1) + capital_in(m) + net_fees(m) + yield(m) − claims_paid(m) − withdrawals(m)
surplus(m)           = max(0, balance before withdrawal − required(m))
surplus_or_shortfall = balance(m) − required(m)

// withdrawals (only ever from surplus, like strict FIFO redemptions)
none                 = 0
surplus above buffer = max(0, surplus(m) − buffer × required(m))
share of net gain    = min(surplus(m), share × max(0, net_gain(m)))

// expected claims per lease per year (event level)
arrears_leg          = min(12 × delinquency, arrears_ceiling) × rent
evictions            = eviction_rate × min(eviction_cost, exit_ceiling) × rent
exit_debts           = A: other_exit_debts × rent · B: (other_exit_debts − penalty) × rent · C: 0
claims_to_fees       = expected_claims / (rent × fee × fee_periods_per_year)

// at-a-glance answers
leases_balance_backs = floor(balance(N) / required_per_lease)
new_leases_per_month_from_gain = net_gain(N) / required_per_lease
raise_to_avoid_block = smallest monthly raise (R$1.000 steps, same window) with 0 blocked months
capital_for_target   = growth_target × required_per_lease − balance(N)
payback_month        = first m with Σ net_gain ≥ starting_capital (projected at month-N pace if beyond N)
return_on_required   = (annual net fees − expected_claims) / required_per_lease + tesouro_share × tesouro_rate

// reserve yield (simulated)
avg_balance          = mean opening(m)
reserve_yield        = Σ (net_fees − claims_paid) / avg_balance × 12/N  +  Σ tesouro_yield / avg_balance × 12/N
return_on_capital    = Σ net_gain / capital_raised   (annualized × 12/N)
selic_benchmark(m)   = opening(m) × selic / 12       // the same capital at Selic

// Sizing & protection (steady state, book full for a year, reserve = balance(N))
normal_net_gain      = net fees − leases × expected_claims + reserve × tesouro_share × tesouro_rate
bad_year_net_gain    = net fees − leases × tail_per_lease + reserve × tesouro_share × tesouro_rate

    Sources and defaults

    • Pilot parameters: mutav/docs/operation/02-parameters.md on origin/main (reviewed 2026-09-19, the single source of numbers): rent R$2.200, guarantee fee 10% (modelled value inside 9–15% by score), rent-arrears ceiling 12× rent (working value, under legal review), exit ceiling 6× as a separate sublimit, capitalized reserve R$300.000, ~6-month pilot, ~28 new contracts/month to a 170-lease book, reserve ~80% TESOURO. Fee charged 12× a year: agencies are billed one boleto a month (was 12,17 = 365 / 30).
    • Coverage policy: 40-reserve.md / open question 9w: the reserve must hold at least 10% of all active ceilings and 2,0 bad years of claims, with a hard floor of 1,0. At R$39.600 of ceilings per lease the 10% rule binds: R$300.000 supports about 75 leases, and 170 leases need about R$673.200.
    • Delinquency: base 2,01% of annual rent = Superlógica IIL, RS apartments, average of the three latest prints (abr/jun/jul 2026), verified at source 2026-09-08. Stress 2,87% (IIL RS aggregate). Tail 5,93% (Loft IIA RS, 15-day threshold). Applied as an annual rate on annual rent: 12 × rate × rent.
    • Evictions: 12-eviction-cost-south.md. While the fiança is live the art. 59 §1º IX liminar is unavailable, so a forced exit costs about 6× rent (capped at the exit ceiling). If the fiança extinguishes once the arrears ceiling is exhausted, the liminar becomes arguable: base 5,5×, low 2,5×. Eviction rate: 12-eviction-rate-south.md, measured filings, adjusted (not MUTAV data). CNJ DataJud first-instance despejo filings (all 27 state courts, own query 2026-10-02) ÷ IBGE PNAD rented households give 0,5–0,7% of South leases facing a suit per year; about a third end in a court-ordered exit. Adjusted for an underwritten, agency-managed book: low 0,10% / base 0,25% / high 0,60% per year.
    • Other exit debts: 12-exit-costs-non-eviction.md §9, base case per lease-year: damage incl. painting 0,056×; accessory charges 0,024×; early-termination penalty 0,077×; abandonment 0,020×; alterations 0,010×; total ≈ 0,19×. All [inference].
    • Bad-year multiplier 2,33: calibrated to 40-reserve: R$1.894 per lease per year in scope B (R$321,9k / 170) against R$813 of expected claims.
    • Debts covered: A = all tenant debts incl. the termination penalty; B = excluding the penalty (default); C = evictions and abandonment only. In C, abandonment is handled through the eviction event (despejo / imissão, art. 66).
    • Take rate 20%: simulator default; the value is still open (06-business-model). Lease term 30 months: typical lease (06, PY:109). TESOURO 11,58%: Etherfuse on-chain accrual, unconfirmed. Selic 13,75%.
    • Changed vs earlier versions: delinquency 2,46% → 2,01%, rent R$2.000 → R$2.200, fee 12% → 10% per 30-day period, ceilings 3+6 → 12+6, eviction rate 2% prior → 0,25% measured-adjusted, single loss ratio → event-level claims (claims-to-fees ratio). The reserve is a simulated balance; "fixed capital" sizing is replaced by the coverage policy plus explicit capital inputs. 2026-10-02 clarity pass: answers first, sizing-rule switch moved into Sizing & protection, the "fiança extinguishes" toggle removed (it only reset the eviction cost; set that directly).

    Simplifications

    • Claims are paid as a smooth expected monthly flow; they do not reduce remaining cover. No subrogation recoveries, no TESOURO price moves, constant rates.
    • Yield accrues on the opening balance at TESOURO share × rate / 12; the liquid sleeve earns nothing.
    • The gate admits as many of the month's planned leases as the surplus allows; blocked leases are not carried over.
    • Selic is the opportunity cost of the same capital, not a target.
