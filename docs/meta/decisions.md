# Decisions

This page indexes every architecture decision behind the MUTAV reserve program. It has three parts:

1. [Architecture decision records](#architecture-decision-records) (ADRs 0001 to 0031) as they stand in [`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/).
2. A [decision log](#decision-log) of the devnet release design, one row per decision, each pointing to the ADR that records it.
3. A pointer to the [open questions and possible improvements](open-questions.md): what is still undecided, and what is deliberately deferred.

Where a newer ADR changes an older one, the index says so. The older ADR keeps its text, because ADRs are never edited after acceptance except for their status ([ADR 0001](../decisions/0001-record-architecture-decisions.md)). The newer decision wins.

## How decisions are made

- Every change to economic behaviour needs a new ADR and a matching update to [`spec.md`](../spec.md) in the same pull request. Economic behaviour means reserve movements, NAV, coverage, free capital, gates, caps and roles.
- An ADR has three sections: **Context**, **Decision** and **Consequences**.
- A reversal is a new ADR that supersedes the old one.
- **Proposed** means written and pending founder confirmation. **Accepted** means confirmed.
- Items that are not decided yet are tracked in [Open questions and possible improvements](open-questions.md), not in an ADR.

## Decision log

Decisions for the devnet release, taken in the design walkthrough of 2026-10-09 and the follow-up passes of 2026-10-10. Later entries supersede earlier ones. "Recorded in" names the ADR; "Governs" names the spec sections each decision changes.
