# BRL currencies on Solana

> **Research date:** 2026-10-01. **Curated:** 2026-10-10. Prior research by the MUTAV team, kept as written except for the edits noted in the [research index](README.md). Notes headed *Since this research* record later decisions. Not legal or investment advice.

*Synthesized from five research notes (BRL stablecoin inventory, Nora Finance and BRS, BRL DeFi and yield rails, Brazilian regulation and Drex, analogues and co-build patterns). Evidence tags: **[verified on-chain]** = read directly from Solana RPC or a live public API on 2026-10-01; **[official]** = the issuer's or regulator's own site, docs or text; **[secondary]** = press, aggregators, third-party repos, founder posts; **[inference]** = analysis, not stated by any source. USD/BRL reference: 5.18 ([Etherfuse rate lookup](https://api.etherfuse.com/lookup/exchange_rate/usd_to_brl)) [verified on-chain].*

## Solana's BRL layer is thin

On Solana, real BRL money is scarce. Leave aside the legacy BRZ float of about 200M, which no DEX route reaches. What remains of the live BRL economy is under **R$2M**: Etherfuse TESOURO at about R$568k, BRD at about R$757k, the new Token-2022 BRZ at about R$433k, and Nora's BRS at just **2,899.68 tokens** [verified on-chain]. There is **no two-sided BRL DEX liquidity**. The only executable exit is an Orca TESOURO→USDC pool at about NAV −2.4%. The only BRL credit market is a Loopscale vault of about R$82k, and Etherfuse's BRL/PIX ramp is still "Upcoming" ([Etherfuse fees](https://docs.etherfuse.com/fees.md)) [official].

For MUTAV, that scarcity means every rail it needs has to come from issuer APIs and off-chain PIX partners, not from on-chain markets. A R$100k pilot reserve would also hold more than 30 times the entire Solana BRS supply of the research date [inference]. Several things work today with documented interfaces: minting BRS by PIX through Nora's minter API, paying claims through MUTAV's own payments account with a Nora offramp, and marking TESOURO from Etherfuse's on-chain `BondPrice` account. Earning a true Selic yield, a BRS↔TESOURO swap, a reserve-seeded pool and reserve shares as collateral all wait on something: an Etherfuse BRL rail, Nora program changes, or regulatory clarity. That clarity covers Brazil's still-unlicensed stablecoin issuers, the BCB's 2026 FX classification of fiat-referenced token trades, and the CVM's view of yield.

One point needed correcting at the time. MUTAV's design documents called TESOURO "Selic-linked". The evidence does not support that, because it accrues at **11.58% against a 13.75% Selic**.

## Seven conflicts in the notes, reconciled where the evidence allows

The notes disagreed in five places flagged up front and in two more found during synthesis.

| Conflict | Verdict | Status |
|---|---|---|
| IOF on stablecoins: "3.5% applies" vs "consultation suspended, none applies" | No stablecoin-specific IOF is in force; the 3.5% figure was a proposed decree | Mostly resolved; residual risk is open |
| BRZ: legacy `FtgGS…` 200M vs new Token-2022 `BRZbFNQD…` 433k | Both mints exist and both readings are correct; the legacy float is stranded from DEX routing, and Loopscale uses the new mint | Facts resolved; Transfero's intent is unresolved |
| TESOURO's underlying: LTN vs NTN/Selic vs "Prefixado 2027"; maturity 2026-10-01 | The on-chain rate rules out a plain Selic-tracking LFT at current pricing; fixed-rate with series rolls fits best | Unresolved; needs written confirmation from Etherfuse |
| BRS "confidential balances" vs classic SPL mint | The BRS mint cannot support Token-2022 confidential transfers; any confidentiality must come from an app layer (possibly Cloak) | Partly resolved; Nora must clarify |
| Nora devnet mint: `BRS2CELW…` vs `BRSx…` on devnet | Use `BRS2CELW…`; `BRSx…` on devnet is an empty placeholder | Resolved |
| Selic level: 15% (inventory note) vs 13.75% (DeFi note) | 13.75% after the 2026-09-16 Copom cut; the 15% figure is uncited and stale | Resolved |
| BRD: "Licensed by BCB" vs "SPSAV authorization in progress" | Treat it as unlicensed pending proof | Unresolved |

**IOF.** The inventory note cites secondary sources ([Blue Consult](https://blueconsult.com.br/resolucoes-bcb-520-521-o-que-muda/), [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/resolucao-do-banco-central-limita-uso-de-stablecoins-no-brasil-investidor-deve-se-preocupar-rens/)) saying Res. BCB 521 applies 3.5% IOF to foreign-currency stablecoin operations [secondary]. The BCB's own press release on Res. 519–521 describes the FX classification and the US$100k cap, but no IOF ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/20918/nota)) [official]. The regulation note traces the 3.5% figure to a **Finance Ministry/Receita decree consultation** in February 2026. That proposal targeted USD stablecoins and exempted individuals up to R$10k a month ([Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/coluna/2026/02/governo-quer-taxar-criptomoedas-com-iof-o-que-isso-muda-para-o-investidor/)) [secondary]. The consultation was then **suspended** ([Passageiro de Primeira](https://passageirodeprimeira.com/governo-federal-vai-suspender-consulta-publica-sobre-iof-de-35-para-stablecoins-e-criptoativos/)) [secondary]. A central-bank resolution does not set tax rates, so the inventory note most likely conflated the proposed decree with the FX classification in Res. 521 [inference]. The verdict is that **no stablecoin-specific IOF applies today**. Two things remain open. First, the BCB's May 2026 note calling stablecoins "private currency" revives the IOF-câmbio argument ([Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/bc-diz-que-stablecoins-nao-sao-criptomoedas-e-revive-ameaca-do-iof/)) [secondary]. Second, the notes never establish whether the FX classification of token trades already pulls existing IOF-câmbio rules onto any leg. BRL-to-BRS conversions are the least exposed legs, and USDC legs (such as buying TESOURO) are the most exposed [inference].

**BRZ.** Both notes are right about different mints. The legacy mint `FtgGSFADXBtroxq8VCausXRr2of47QBf5AS1NtZCu4GD` is classic SPL with 4 decimals, **200,009,925.55 supply** and no freeze authority. Its mint authority is a bare keypair [verified on-chain]. rwa.xyz still counts it as about 72% of all BRZ ([rwa.xyz BRZ](https://app.rwa.xyz/assets/BRZ)) [secondary]. The new mint `BRZbFNQDcWLfcdHmAkqEVnLHCAWKTRf6eHyEaWdZp3JN` is Token-2022 with 6 decimals and **433,345.01 supply**, and its metadata is hosted on a Transfero-named blob store [verified on-chain]. Loopscale's BRZ vault uses the new mint as its principal ([Loopscale API](https://tars.loopscale.com/v1/markets/lending_vaults/info)) [verified on-chain], and Jupiter's quote API returns `TOKEN_NOT_TRADABLE` for the legacy mint [verified on-chain]. The Jupiter *token search* still lists the legacy mint with 736 holders, and that is not a contradiction: it indexes the token, it does not route it. The supported reading is that Transfero has moved its active Solana issuance to the new mint and left a large, idle legacy float [inference]. What the notes cannot settle is whether the legacy 200M is redeemable, held in a treasury, or due to be burned, because holder concentration was blocked by RPC rate limits. **Unresolved:** which mint Transfero redeems by PIX, and the fate of the legacy supply.

**TESOURO's underlying.** Four descriptions compete. CoinGecko says LTN zero-coupon fixed-rate bonds ([CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/etherfuse-tesouro)) [secondary]. The Etherfuse app page says "NTN", with maturity **2026-10-01** and accrual "based on the Selic rate" ([Etherfuse app](https://app.etherfuse.com/bonds/BRNTNaZeTJANz9PeuD8drNbBHwGgg7ZTjiQYrFgWQ48p)) [official, as summarized by a fetch tool]. A third-party integrator calls it "Tesouro Prefixado 2027" at about 13.56% ([safenudge #56](https://github.com/davigiroux/safenudge.xyz/issues/56)) [secondary]. The chain itself says **1158 bps**, last updated 2026-09-24 [verified on-chain]. With Selic at 13.75% ([DGABC](https://www.dgabc.com.br/Noticia/4347352/copom-reduz-taxa-selic-em-0-25-ponto-porcentual-para-13-75-ao-ano)) [secondary], a Selic-tracking LFT would accrue close to 13.75%. An 11.58% rate fits a fixed-rate book priced at older auction yields, or a Selic accrual net of a spread [inference]. The 2026-10-01 maturity was the research date, and "Prefixado 2027" could be the next series. Together they suggest TESOURO **rolls between series**, so both its underlying and its rate can change at each roll [inference]. **Unresolved.** Until Etherfuse confirms in writing, TESOURO is best described as "tokenized Brazilian federal bond exposure at the rate published on-chain", not as Selic exposure.

**BRS confidential balances.** Nora's site claims "confidential balances on Solana" ([nora.finance](https://www.nora.finance/)) [official]. The Solana BRS mint `BRSxQRUaGswjLs7ewcH7uXj3r7SgmfKSSLLXyCKHZtUo` is owned by the classic SPL Token program [verified on-chain], and confidential transfers are a Token-2022 extension, so the mint cannot provide them. One partial reconciliation exists. Solana Compass lists Nora as one of three protocols running **Cloak**, a Solana privacy layer, "in production" ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/projects/cloak)) [secondary]. Confidentiality would then live at the application or wrapper layer, not in the token [inference]. The new BRZ mint, by contrast, carries `confidentialTransferMint` on-chain [verified on-chain]. **Partly resolved.** A public reserve should not hold a privacy-wrapped balance, given MUTAV's principle that only on-chain-verifiable holdings count.

**Nora devnet mint.** The docs name `BRS2CELW6Cueo2mrMUVvAr5GDT7Pw8TeostC2JLMpBk4` as the sandbox/devnet mint ([Nora BRS token docs](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/core-concepts/brs-token)) [official]. On devnet it had 79,122.49 supply and its own mint and freeze authorities [verified on-chain]. The mainnet address `BRSx…` also exists on devnet, but with **0 supply**, and both its authorities are the mainnet admin key [verified on-chain]. It is a reserved vanity address, not the sandbox [inference]. **Resolved:** MUTAV's devnet tests use `BRS2CELW…` and the separate devnet program `9fBSeVHUCaHHUzkktiRp5Yn35emxx3S1ERzn7oHsi8je`. Any code that hardcodes `BRSx…` on devnet will silently test against an empty mint.

**Selic and BRD.** The inventory note's "Selic is 15%" has no citation and predates the September 2026 cuts, so this page uses 13.75%. BRD's site claims both "Licensed by Brazil's Central Bank" and that its SPSAV authorization is "in progress" ([brd.digital](https://brd.digital)) [official]. Both cannot be true in the same sense, so BRD is best treated as **not yet authorized** until it shows the authorization.

## Nora is a programmable, early, single-key issuer

**Who they are.** Nora Finance, Inc. is São Paulo-based, and its policies name its leadership team ([AML/CFT policy](https://www.nora.finance/legal/aml-cft), [KYC/KYB policy](https://www.nora.finance/legal/kyc-kyb)) [official]. Nora describes itself as a **pure issuer that is not a PSAV** and "not subject to prior authorization" from the BCB. It voluntarily files with COAF, and **pushes all end-user KYC onto its minters** ([KYC/KYB §1](https://www.nora.finance/legal/kyc-kyb), [AML/CFT §2](https://www.nora.finance/legal/aml-cft)) [official]. Its policy cites "BCB Resolution no. 519/2024", while the BCB published Res. 519 on 10 Nov 2025 ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/20918/nota)) [official].

**How BRS moves.** Only KYB-approved legal entities, the Nora Authorized Minters (NAMs), can mint or burn. Onboarding has six stages, and the "Medium" risk tier covers "DeFi customers" and entities "in regulatory authorization process" ([KYC/KYB §5–6](https://www.nora.finance/legal/kyc-kyb)) [official]. To mint, a NAM calls `POST /v2/intents/onramp` and receives a PIX BR Code that expires in about 30 minutes. Once the PIX lands, Nora mints to a whitelisted wallet on `solana`, `ethereum`, `base` or `polygon`, with a minimum of R$5 ([onramp docs](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/onramp-intent)) [official]. To redeem, the NAM calls `POST /v2/intents/offramp`. The holder then signs a `user_burn` transaction that Nora stages after issuing an attested `grant_burn`, and Nora pays by PIX only to keys on the organization's own list. **CPF keys and other companies' CNPJs are rejected** ([offramp docs](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/offramp-intent), [burn signing](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/burn-signing)) [official]. A wallet needs an on-chain whitelist grant before Nora will mint to it or burn from it. The NAM's own "whitelister" key signs these grants, the target `user` account does not sign, and each grant costs about 0.002 SOL ([wallet whitelisting](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/wallet-whitelisting)) [official]. No fee schedule is published. An older "zero fees" claim survives only in search snippets [secondary]. Settlement is claimed as "finality in seconds" on the homepage ([nora.finance](https://www.nora.finance/)) [official], but a search snippet of the same site says "settled via PIX in a day" [secondary], and the offramp's `scheduled` state comes with no completion guarantee [official].

**Where control sits.** The on-chain reads show a concentration any BRS holder must price in. One plain system wallet is at once the **BRS freeze authority, the upgrade authority of the minting program, and the admin in its config** [verified on-chain]. The mint authority is a 186-byte config account owned by `noRAkuzH…`. Its decoded fields point to a backend minter hot key, a burn attester, a 5 BRS minimum, a **500k BRS per-mint maximum and a 10M BRS daily cap** [verified on-chain bytes; field meanings are inference]. Nora can `pause()` the token or a single pathway during an incident ([bridging docs](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/bridging)) [official], but there is no published freeze policy, program audit, IDL or SDK. The GitHub org has two asset repos only ([brs-assets](https://github.com/Nora-Finance/brs-assets)) [official]. Under classic SPL, a freeze lands on a specific token account, so **one key could freeze a reserve's BRS account** [inference].

> **Since this research:** the program counts a frozen reserve account as zero liquid BRS and refuses new guarantees while it is frozen ([ADR 0020](../decisions/0020-operator-admin-separation-and-recovery.md)). The issuer's single-key freeze authority is listed in [known limitations](../security/known-limitations.md), to be raised with the issuer before mainnet.

**Footprint.** About 94% of BRS supply lives on Base (24,486.70) and Polygon (26,509.80) as a LayerZero v2 OFT. Solana sits **outside that mesh, with no bridge** ([bridging docs](https://www.nora.finance/docs/integrate/flows/bridging)) [official; supplies verified on-chain]. Total supply of about R$54k against a homepage "TPV 100K+" ([nora.finance](https://www.nora.finance/)) suggests cumulative flow, not float [inference]. Reserves are stated as federal bonds plus bank CDBs, custodians are unnamed, and the Fact Finance proof-of-reserves page is client-rendered and could not be read ([Fact Finance](https://fact.finance/en/reserves/nora/)) [official]. The partner logos are 4Pay, OZAV, Pods, GOWD, Uniswap Foundation, Pinheiro Neto, Fact Finance, Conta.vc and Decentral [official]. Pods, one of those partners, already exposes a public **Pix BRL ↔ BRS Solana** rail backed 1:1 by Avenia BRLA inventory, with no markup ([Pods changelog](https://docs.pods.finance/changelog)) [official].

**Roadmap.** Nora's own KYB policy names **sBRS**, a yield-bearing BRS that waits on PL 4.308/2024 to allow reserve-yield distribution "without classification as a security" ([KYC/KYB §2](https://www.nora.finance/legal/kyc-kyb)) [official]. A founder post targets a BRL product at 18% APY or more and a hedged USD product at 10% ([Cioffi Substack](https://cioffi.substack.com/p/nubank-was-the-warm-up-brazils-3rd)) [secondary]. With Selic at 13.75%, 18% would need leverage, credit risk or subsidy [inference].

**What an integrator could build with Nora.** Each item below is an [inference] grounded in Nora's documented interfaces:

- **NAM status.** A NAM gets direct PIX-to-BRS minting. A permissioned, allowlisted design sits outside the enhanced diligence Nora's AML policy reserves for "DeFi protocols without permissioned access" ([AML/CFT §7.3](https://www.nora.finance/legal/aml-cft)) [official].
- **Hardening.** Moving the single admin key to a multisig with a time lock, plus a published IDL and audit.
- **CPI and PDA support.** Making `user_burn` callable by CPI, so a reserve PDA can redeem directly, and confirming that the mint API accepts an off-curve PDA as `destinationWallet`.
- **Beneficiary Pix-key registry.** A registry for claim payments would let a single offramp pay the agency's key.
- **On-chain reserve oracle.** Fact Finance publishing its BRS reserve attestation to an on-chain account, which a program could read instead of taking issuer backing on trust.
- **Distribution.** A Solana BRS liquidity and bridge program (OFT adapter or NTT).

## Seven opportunities, made concrete for the MUTAV reserve

**BRS↔TESOURO paths.** Today there is no direct path. Etherfuse's primary market takes USDC (`payment_mint` = USDC in its cost API) [verified on-chain], it shows no evidence of accepting BRS, and its BRL rail is not live [official]. The working route is BRS → Nora offramp → BRL in MUTAV's account → FX to USDC through an authorized provider → Etherfuse purchase, and the reverse for redemptions. That is **two FX legs** to hold a BRL asset, which adds spread, FX timing, a possible IOF exposure on the USD legs, and Res. 521 FX classification on both [inference]. The exit is easier: the Orca pool converts TESOURO to USDC at about −2.4%, and Loopscale can lend BRZ against TESOURO in very small size [verified on-chain]. MUTAV's adapter should therefore keep allocation and deallocation admin-driven and asynchronous, through Etherfuse's API. It should not assume an atomic on-chain swap, and it should treat the Orca bid as the floor price in under-coverage scenarios [inference]. The real fix is commercial: either Etherfuse turns on its BRL rail and accepts BRS, or an issuer-run BRS↔USDC desk feeds Etherfuse. In Mexico, Brale's issuer-run swap desk launched MXNe on three chains in **21 days** without waiting for AMM depth ([Brale case study](https://brale.xyz/case-studies/etherfuse)) [official].

> **Since this research:** ADR 0027 (proposed) keeps this shape. The reserve admin runs `allocate` and `deallocate` through its time lock, two-phase moves are capped and booked at a haircut while in transit, a hard BRS liquidity floor protects claims, and TESOURO is priced from Etherfuse's `BondPrice` with an accrual corridor. The TESOURO cap stays at zero until Etherfuse's redemption path and lead time are confirmed ([ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-multi-asset-adapters-liquidity-floor-rebalancing.md), [multi-asset vaults on Solana](multi-asset-vaults-on-solana.md)).

**Earning Selic on the reserve.** No clean on-chain Selic exposure exists on Solana. TESOURO pays 11.58%, against 13.75% for Selic and the 14.00% BRD advertises for sBRD. TESOURO is still the only option whose price MUTAV can verify on-chain, which MUTAV's principle requires [inference]. Value it at `min(BondPrice accrual, accrual curve)` and add a staleness bound wider than the observed seven-day write gap. Plan for roll risk at series maturity, given the 2026-10-01 maturity on the app page [inference]. sBRD has no Solana mint found, conflicting licensing claims, and about R$757k of total float, which rules it out for the pilot [inference]. sBRS does not exist.

> **Since this research:** the pilot does not hold a yield asset. The reserve's income is Nora's issuer revenue on the BRS it holds, swept in through the income inbox ([ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-brs-income-intake.md), [ADR 0018](../decisions/0018-brs-only-pilot-reserve.md)). The reserve is operational infrastructure, not a yield product.

**PIX-native guarantee-fee collection.** Nora's onramp is built for this: a dynamic BR Code, PIX in, BRS minted to a whitelisted wallet [official]. Two frictions shape the design [inference]. First, each BR Code is a one-off with about 30 minutes to live. Recurring tenant fees through PIX Automático therefore have to be collected by MUTAV's own licensed payment provider into its own account, then batch-minted through Nora into the operator wallet and contributed to the reserve net of MUTAV's take. Second, minting straight into the reserve PDA depends on Nora accepting an off-curve destination, which is undocumented. Minting to the operator wallet first avoids that dependency and fits MUTAV's existing operator role. Under Res. 521 (iv), the conversion from BRL to a fiat-referenced token is the FX-classified step [official].

**Instant BRL claim payments.** MUTAV's design pays claims to a whitelisted payments account, and Nora's burn flow needs a wallet that can sign `user_burn` [official]. The two fit without any Nora changes [inference]. The flow runs like this: `pay_claim` → payments wallet (whitelisted with Nora) → `prepare-burn` → sign → `approve-burn` → PIX to MUTAV's own CNPJ key → PIX to the agency → `settle_payout(pix_e2e_hash)`. The extra hop exists because Nora rejects other companies' CNPJ keys. A Nora beneficiary-key registry would remove it, and Avenia's Solana token-to-PIX endpoint already accepts arbitrary keys, though which tokens it supports is undocumented [official]. Speed is the open variable: Nora claims finality in seconds, but its `scheduled` state carries no service level. MUTAV's ten-day settlement SLA leaves plenty of slack, and an agency-facing promise of "instant" should wait for measured sandbox timings [inference]. Pilot claim calls of up to R$10k (about US$1.9k) fall well under both Res. 521's US$100k per-operation cap and PL 4.308's proposed US$10k 24-hour hold threshold [inference].

> **Since this research:** the payout SLA left the program. `settle_payout` stays as the settlement record, and service levels live in the operator platform. MUTAV may also pay the agency first from its own bank account and be reimbursed by the reserve through `pay_claim`, which pays exactly the filed amount; above the per-call cap, the reserve admin approves the amount first ([ADR 0021](../decisions/0021-claims-approval-amendment-exact-payment.md)).

**A reserve-seeded BRS pool.** Solana has no BRS pool, and MUTAV would be the main holder. Seeding liquidity would help price discovery, but it also means MUTAV making a market in its own reserve asset [inference]. A BRS/USDC pool is an FX position, not a stable pair. If BRL strengthens, the pool swaps BRS out for USDC worth fewer reais against BRL obligations [inference]. The safer pattern follows the OnRe and Brale precedents ([OnRe Liquidity Engine](https://solanacompass.com/news/onre-liquidity-engine-launches-for-onyc-capital-backed-rfq-quotes-routed-by-titan), [Brale](https://brale.xyz/case-studies/etherfuse)) [secondary]/[official]. The issuer runs the primary RFQ. MUTAV places only a single-sided BRS ladder on Meteora DLMM ([Meteora DLMM docs](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dlmm/strategies-and-use-cases)) [official], paid for from *excess* reserve and capped below surplus. It marks the position at withdrawable value with an FX haircut, so the coverage gate sees the position's real value [inference]. Even then, the pool would not reach the 94% of BRS supply on EVM until Nora bridges Solana [official].

**Reserve shares as collateral.** OnRe is the closest analogue. Its regulated reinsurance balance sheet became ONyc, mintable with USDG, then Kamino collateral with USDG borrow rebates, then tranched on Exponent ([OnRe/USDG](https://www.onre.finance/blog/onre-integrates-usdg-to-expand-real-world-yield-access-on-solana), [OnRe/Kamino](https://www.onre.finance/blog/unlocking-more-utility-onyc-joins-kamino-multiply)) [official]. Kamino prices collateral through Scope over Pyth, Switchboard, Chainlink and RedStone, and runs isolated and RWA "Prime" markets ([eco.com Kamino explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14801186-kamino-lending-solana-s-money-market-explained)) [secondary]. MUTAV's share differs in one important way: its **NAV gaps down on claims**. Listing it would need claim-aware NAV publication, a senior/junior split with the junior taking first loss, exit liquidity, and a BRS borrow side [inference]. Allowlisted, KYC'd holders also clash with permissionless lending markets. A Solana Attestation Service credential plus a transfer hook could gate the share ([SAS](https://solana.com/news/solana-attestation-service)) [official], but many venues restrict hooked tokens [inference, not source-checked]. This is a post-pilot item. See also the [OnRe comparable](onre-comparable.md).

> **Since this research:** shares are to be transfer-restricted on mainnet, with the mechanism chosen before the mainnet `initialize` ([open questions](../meta/open-questions.md)).

**Co-issuance or yield-bearing BRS.** On Solana it is technically easy. A separate Token-2022 mint with **Scaled UI Amount** lets the issuer move a multiplier from realized reserve yield. That extension cannot be combined with InterestBearing ([Solana docs](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount)) [official], and it is safer than InterestBearing because the displayed balance never runs ahead of what the reserve actually earned [inference]. A MUTAV-branded "guarantee BRL" wrapper could follow the M^0 extension pattern: lock the base token and mint 1:1, with yield routed by rule. M^0 itself is USD-only and forbids transfer hooks on extensions ([m0 extensions](https://github.com/m0-foundation/solana-m-extensions)) [official], so a BRS wrapper would be custom code [inference]. The more practical near-term economics come from Open USD's "pays its distributors" model, which launched on Solana on 2026-09-30 ([Solana news](https://solana.com/news/open-usd-is-live-on-solana)) [official]: an issuer shares reserve yield on the float a distributor holds, and that yield flows into the distributor's reserve as income rather than to holders [inference]. A yield-bearing token offered to the public is blocked until PL 4.308 passes and the CVM's Parecer 40 position is addressed [official]/[inference].

> **Since this research:** the program refuses a reserve mint with `ScaledUiAmount` or `InterestBearingConfig`, because a balance multiplier never reaches the raw balances NAV is computed from. Issuer income arrives as BRS through the income inbox instead ([ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-brs-income-intake.md)).

**Addendum (2026-10-01): BRS/TESOURO pool on Polygon.** A **BRS/TESOURO pool exists on Polygon** (Uniswap v4, 0.04% fee tier, about US$1.2k TVL). TESOURO's Polygon token is `0xd574b191B5a00262Ff19953d3CE25543AB7C8098`. Nora's CEO announced it on X on 2026-09-30, saying an independent LP created it, not Nora or Etherfuse [secondary: a founder post, seen as a screenshot, no URL recorded (unverified)]. DexScreener doesn't index this v4 pool, but it confirms TESOURO's Polygon address and a TESOURO/USDC v3 pool of about $986. **There is still no BRS/TESOURO pool on Solana.** The Polygon pool is a precedent for a jointly seeded Solana pool.

## Brazil regulates the conversion legs, not the on-chain reserve

This section summarizes public rules as found on the research date. It is not legal advice, and MUTAV's own legal analysis is kept private.

**VASP rules.** Res. BCB 519, 520 and 521 took effect on **2 Feb 2026**. They require BCB authorization for virtual-asset services, either as an existing institution or as an SPSAV (intermediária, custodiante or corretora) ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/20918/nota)) [official]. Existing operators must file by **30 Oct 2026** ([VBSO](https://vbso.com.br/as-sociedades-prestadoras-de-servicos-de-ativos-virtuais-e-o-prazo-de-30-de-outubro-de-2026/)) [secondary]. Minimum capital runs from about R$10.8M to R$37.2M, client assets must be segregated, custodians need proof of reserves, and algorithmic stablecoins are barred ([Lefosse](https://lefosse.com/noticias/alerta/banco-central-regulamenta-o-uso-de-ativos-virtuais-e-o-funcionamento-das-prestadoras-de-servicos-de-ativos-virtuais/), [NDM](https://ndmadvogados.com.br/artigo/capital-social-minimo-psav-e-exchange/)) [secondary]. Nora's KYB asks minters for PSAV authorization "where applicable" [official].

**Stablecoin and FX rules.** Res. 521 treats four activities as FX: international payments with virtual assets, settling international card obligations, transfers to or from **self-custody wallets** (the owner must be identified), and the purchase, sale or exchange of **fiat-referenced** tokens. Operations with non-FX-authorized counterparties are capped at **US$100k**, and reporting has been mandatory since 4 May 2026 ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/20918/nota)) [official]. The text does not separate BRL-pegged from USD-pegged tokens. Read literally, BRS trades are FX, although the economic purpose argues otherwise; this is unresolved [inference]. Res. BCB 561 bars eFX providers from settling abroad in crypto from **1 Oct 2026** ([Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/455149/advogado-avalia-norma-do-bc-que-nao-proibe-stablecoins-mas-limita-efx)) [secondary]. Brazil has **no stablecoin-issuer licensing regime**. PL 4.308/2024 is still in committee, and its debated text would allow a 24-hour hold on transfers above US$10k abroad or to self-custody ([Câmara](https://www.camara.leg.br/proposicoesWeb/fichadetramitacao?idProposicao=2467716), [Blue Consult](https://blueconsult.com.br/trava-24-horas-stablecoins/)) [official]/[secondary]. The BCB's May 2026 note argues stablecoins are "private currency" ([Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/bc-diz-que-stablecoins-nao-sao-criptomoedas-e-revive-ameaca-do-iof/)) [secondary]. That puts pressure on "pure issuer, not a PSAV" positions and on any yield-bearing BRL token [inference]. The IOF status is reconciled above.

**Securities and tax.** Under CVM Parecer 40, a token offering profit from others' efforts is likely a security or collective investment contract ([CVM](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/pare040.html)) [official, not re-verified this session]. The CVM's DLT pilot draft reached its board on 17 Sep 2026 and is not yet in force ([Bitnotícias](https://bitnoticias.com.br/noticias/cvm-publica-minuta-para-tokenizacao-e-libera-mercado/)) [secondary]. MP 1.303's flat 17.5% tax lapsed in October 2025, so crypto gains remain exempt up to R$35k of monthly sales and are taxed at 15–22.5% above that ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/minhas-financas/mp-1-303-cai-no-congresso-veja-como-fica-a-tributacao-dos-investimentos-agora/)) [secondary].

**Drex.** In August 2025 the BCB dropped DLT from Drex's next phase on privacy grounds. It refocused the project on a centralized collateral-lien (gravames) system rolling out from 2H 2026, with no public-chain interoperability planned ([Seu Dinheiro](https://www.seudinheiro.com/2025/economia/drex-abandonara-blockchain-na-proxima-fase-para-entregar-solucao-em-2026-diz-jornal-gmda/), [HRSA](https://hrsa.com.br/artigo/drex-2026-garantias-reconciliacao-de-gravames/)) [secondary]. Private BRL tokens therefore face no official on-chain competitor before 2027, and there is no sovereign on-chain BRL to hold instead [inference].

**PIX.** PIX has no crypto-specific ban. Res. 520 does push PSAVs toward payment-institution-grade accounts, and unauthorized payment institutions reportedly face a R$15k per-transfer cap ([Let's Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/pix/resolucao-520-psavs-pix)) [secondary].

## Open questions for the issuers

Factual points the research could not settle from public sources:

- **Nora:** whether the onramp accepts an off-curve PDA as `destinationWallet`; whether `user_burn` can be invoked by CPI; when the single admin key moves to a multisig with a time lock, and the written freeze policy; whether beneficiary Pix keys can be registered for claim payments, and the measured time from `approve-burn` to PIX credit; what "confidential balances on Solana" means for a classic SPL mint; the reserve custodians and the bond/CDB split, and whether Fact Finance can publish the attestation on-chain; whether a Solana↔EVM bridge is planned.
- **Etherfuse:** TESOURO's exact underlying (LTN, NTN-F or LFT) and roll schedule; why the on-chain rate sits below Selic, and whether an LFT stablebond is planned; the `BondPrice` layout and update cadence; whether purchase and redeem are permissionless or KYC-gated and callable by a program PDA; when the BRL/PIX ramp goes live and whether it will accept BRS; redemption mechanics and limits, including a reported "instant within float, T+1 beyond" claim (unverified); who provides the Orca bid near NAV −2.4%; what governs the 1-of-2 mint authority.

## Conclusion

The BRL-on-Solana question turned out to be less about choosing an asset than about who builds the missing rails. Every BRL token on Solana is either tiny, idle, or reachable only through an issuer API. MUTAV's design suits that world: asynchronous allocations, claim payments routed through a payments wallet, a reserve that counts only what it can verify on-chain. Those constraints, built in for safety, also match how Nora and Etherfuse actually work. The real risks are therefore not market liquidity. They are three single points of control: Nora's single admin key, Etherfuse's 1-of-2 mint authority, and a TESOURO underlying that nobody had confirmed in writing.
