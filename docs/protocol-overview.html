MUTAV Protocol Overview

An on-chain reserve on Solana that backs rental guarantees (fiança) in Brazil.

MUTAV is an institutional fiador. It gives Brazilian tenants a paid rental guarantee through the real-estate agencies that already run their leases, so no personal guarantor or cash deposit is needed. Every guarantee is backed by a BRL reserve held by a Solana program. The program records the cover of every active guarantee, refuses any capital movement that would leave that cover under-backed, and never blocks a claim payment for solvency reasons. Anyone can recompute the reserve's health from public state. The reserve is operational infrastructure that keeps the guarantees healthy. It is not a yield product.

1The problem: rental guarantees in Brazil

Brazilian rental law lets a landlord ask for exactly one guarantee per lease (Lei 8.245/91, art. 37). That one guarantee is the landlord's only protection. In 2024 Brazil had 17.8 million rented households (IBGE, PNAD Contínua).

The usual options fail someone. A personal guarantor needs a friend or relative with a paid-off property. A cash deposit locks up to three months of rent for the whole lease. Fintech guarantors are cheap and fast, but their reserves are opaque. One of them, QuintoCred, stopped writing contracts in June 2025 and left about 45,000 contracts and 3,000 partner agencies without a guarantor.

We interviewed seven agency leaders in the South of Brazil. Five of the seven front the landlord's rent themselves while they wait for the guarantor to pay. They cite unilateral cancellation, waits of 60 to 90 days, partial payments and no visibility.

Agencies don't distrust guarantees. They distrust guarantors, because they can't see the reserve or the payment clock.

MUTAV makes the reserve public and bound by rules. A segregated bank account shows a balance once a month and cannot stop its owner in between. A program can: capital leaves only from surplus over the cover it backs, and every guarantee, provision and claim payment is an on-chain record anyone can check.

2How MUTAV works

Agencies never sign on-chain. They work in the MUTAV platform as they do today. MUTAV runs every chain touchpoint as the operator, and a separate Reserve Admin sets the limits the operator works within.

  1. Register. When a lease is signed, the operator registers the guarantee with its cover: the R$ ceiling of the fiança. The solvency gate refuses the registration if the reserve can't back the new cover.
  2. Guarantee fees. The tenant pays the guarantee fee with the rent. Each agency pays MUTAV one bill a month. The operator books it with contribute_fees: the net amount goes to the reserve and raises the value of every share, and MUTAV's take goes to a separate treasury account. Fees never mint shares.
  3. Claims. When a tenant defaults, the agency sends a payment request. The operator files a claim on-chain, and the program sets the amount aside as a provision at once. Then pay_claim sends BRS to MUTAV's payments account, MUTAV pays the agency by Pix, and the agency pays the landlord. Each claim is one event, such as one month's rent.
  4. Capital. Capital providers hold reserve shares. Deposits and redemptions go through a first-in, first-out queue and are filled at the on-chain share value. Redemptions are paid only from surplus over what the guarantees need. During the pilot the reserve runs on MUTAV's own capital and is not open to public investment.

MUTAV also has a partnership with Nora, the issuer of BRS. Nora shares issuer revenue with its partners, and MUTAV routes that income into the reserve with sweep_income. It is booked apart from guarantee fees and never enters the coverage math.

3The reserve in numbers

The reserve does not hold the full cover of every lease. It holds a share of it, the coverage ratio c, plus a stress buffer for the claims that could be filed next. Filed claims are always held in full.

coverage_required = max( c × remaining cover,
                         provisions + min(stress buffer, unprovisioned cover) )

The gate checks this before new guarantees and redemptions. It never checks it before a claim payment. If claims push the reserve below target, new guarantees and redemptions stop until capital comes in or the book runs off. Claims keep being paid.

4Why it is safe

Operator and admin are separate

The operator runs contracts, agencies and the whole guarantee and claim cycle. The Reserve Admin looks after the reserve's financial health and security through ceilings. It does not judge contracts. A separate pauser can stop the reserve and revoke the operator at once.

Roles change hands in two steps: the new key must accept, so a typo can't appoint a dead address.

Claims are never blocked by the solvency gate

The gate protects the reserve from capital moving out. It never refuses a claim payment, and a pause doesn't stop claims either. Only the filed claim, the caps, liquid BRS or a frozen reserve can stop one.

MUTAV can also pay the agency by Pix from its bank first, and the reserve then reimburses MUTAV's payments account. Landlords are paid on time even while a large claim waits for approval.

A compromised operator has a hard ceiling

The most a stolen operator key can cost is the admin-set claim caps. Claim payments are the only irreversible operator action. Everything else can be undone by another operator action: a registration by a void close, a close by reinstating the guarantee, a filed claim by withdrawing it.

Recovery: freeze, appoint a new operator, reconcile the platform's records with on-chain events, and correct with ordinary operator actions.

Multisig with time locks

The Reserve Admin is a Squads v4 multisig. Two multisigs with the same members hold authority: the admin multisig has a short time lock, and the upgrade multisig a long one. Every admin action is time-locked except pausing.

Up to three guardian keys, set by the admin, can pause and do nothing else.

Donations can't move the share value

The program tracks the amounts it moved, not raw token balances. BRS sent straight to a reserve account doesn't change the share value. Anyone can sweep it into a separate account for unsolicited money.

No money gets stuck: the Reserve Admin returns it to the sender or books it into the reserve. Guarantee fees and income wait until the first deposit is filled, so they can't be lost to share rounding.

Transparent by default

Every guarantee, provision, claim payment and capital movement is an on-chain account or event. Anyone can call refresh to recompute coverage and share value. The /reserve page shows it all with no wallet, including tracked against actual balances.

Holders can always exit. An investor removed from the allowlist can still redeem existing shares.

Two more guards: fills are refused while a sharp move in the share value is unexplained by verified inflows, and every account carries reserved space so later features need no migration.

7What's next

  • A security audit, then a capped mainnet pilot with real guarantees.
  • The operator moves from the hand-signed console to MUTAV's platform backend, with a key held in a key-management service.
  • Partial fills at the head of the capital queue. The pilot fills whole requests only.
  • A cumulative baseline for the share-value guard, and a tighter bound for the real pilot.
  • Price protection for capital requests: a minimum amount out.
  • The claim-notice gate, which holds capital fills while a missed-rent notice is open, before any outside capital.
  • The full fiança lifecycle: exoneration notice, claims tail and settlement records. Space is already reserved in the accounts.
  • More reserve assets, such as tokenized Brazilian treasury bonds, through capped adapters.
  • Under discussion: a permissionless exit if the queue waits too long.
