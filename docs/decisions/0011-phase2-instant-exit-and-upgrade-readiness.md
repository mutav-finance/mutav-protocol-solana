# 0011 — Phase-2 instant exit, earmark-aware solvency and upgrade readiness

- **Status:** accepted (2026-10-02; revised the same day after the solvency, Solana and consistency reviews). The instant exit is **designed, not enabled**: it ships disabled and needs a later timelocked upgrade and flag change. Amends ADR 0008 (FIFO-only withdrawals, as a phase-2 exception) and ADR 0009 (the MUTAV capital wallet is now recorded in config).

## Context

The queue (ADRs 0008, 0010) is the only way out of the reserve, and exits wait for the admin. Some holders will want to leave immediately and accept a price for it. OnRe's Prop AMM sell (`onre-finance/onre-sol`, MIT) shows one way: an atomic sell at NAV minus a convex haircut that grows with utilization and recent exit volume, with the haircut kept in the vault. Ported as is, it would break MUTAV's rules:

- OnRe's sells and queued fills draw on **one** vault, so atomic sellers jump the queue.
- It has no solvency gate, no stale-price check, no allowlist and no automatic shut-off.
- Its per-order haircut is not split-proof: five small exits pay much less than one large one.
- A holder who knows a claim is coming could exit before the loss is in NAV.

The pilot must ship first, and the reserve will be live when phase 2 is ready. Phase 2 must not require migrating live accounts or changing what the pilot audited, above all the never-gated `pay_claim` path.

## Decision

1. **Earmark-aware solvency from the pilot.** `surplus = max(0, stable_assets − coverage_required)`; `free_capital = surplus − earmark_eff`; `earmark_eff = min(buffer_earmark, surplus, brs_balance − provisions)`, zero when the `INSTANT_EXIT` flag is clear and zero for a starved queue head. In the pilot the flag is clear and `buffer_earmark = 0`, so `free_capital = surplus`. Every gated outflow and new liability must fit in the `free_capital` computed before it, so queue fills and new guarantees never consume the earmark. The gates carry no headroom term: a term that falls with surplus would let each outflow re-derive a smaller earmark, and the queue could drain it step by step. The earmark is accounting inside `reserve`: it still counts in `stable_assets` and NAV, and `pay_claim` neither reads nor writes it. Only the instructions that already read the price and `free_capital` store the ratchet (`buffer_earmark := earmark_eff`); claim, fee, notice, cancel and claim-of-assets instructions never touch it.
2. **Claims-senior liquidity.** Redemption fills are capped by `liquid_budget = brs_balance − provisions − earmark_eff`, and `allocate` keeps `brs_balance ≥ provisions + earmark_eff`.
3. **Instant exit (phase 2).** `instant_redeem(shares, min_out, proof)` burns shares the signer owns (never as a delegate) and pays `out = raw − haircut` directly from `reserve`, limited to `earmark_eff` and to `stable_assets − out ≥ coverage_required + headroom`. The stored earmark falls by `raw`, so the retained haircut becomes free capital. The haircut is the integral of a convex marginal rate over `x = max(recent exit volume, target − earmark_eff)`, computed as one exact rational rounded up once; both terms of `x` rise by `raw`, which makes it split-proof within an epoch up to rounding. It stays in the reserve and raises NAV for every remaining holder. `out == 0` is rejected, and `out ≥ min_out` is required.
4. **Queue first.** The buffer is funded only from surplus above the headroom and only while the queue is empty (`fund_exit_buffer`, an operator crank; the operator or admin may defund at any time). It is released to the queue if the head waits longer than `buffer_release_after_secs`, and `instant_redeem` must present the head whenever the queue is non-empty, so a starved head blocks instant exit at once. An instant exit never reduces `free_capital`. Instant exit requires surplus to stay above the headroom after the exit, so it switches off well before under-coverage. Clearing the flag returns the earmark to free capital immediately.
5. **Automatic disables.** Instant exit is refused when the `INSTANT_EXIT` flag is off, when paused, in under-coverage, when `fulfil_halted`, on a stale price (tighter bound), while any claim notice is pending, below the headroom, with a starved queue head, or past the caps.
5a. **Claim notices ship in the pilot and gate both queues.** MUTAV is a queue participant and also times admin fills and runs the operator, which learns of a missed rent up to 15 days before `file_claim`. So `flag_claim_notice` / `close_claim_notice` and `VaultState.pending_notices` are pilot features: while a notice is open, `fulfil_redeems`, `fulfil_deposits` and (phase 2) `instant_redeem` refuse. A notice closes only when the claim is `Paid`, the leg is `FullyProvisioned` (provision = the leg's whole remaining cover), or it is `Withdrawn`, because `pay_claim` may pay more than the filed provision. Notices never gate `file_claim`, `pay_claim` or any claim-path instruction.
6. **MUTAV is barred.** `VaultConfig.mutav_capital_wallet` (and an `exit.barred` list for affiliates) cannot instant-exit. MUTAV earns from haircuts only through its shares, like every holder. It exits through the queue.
7. **Holding period.** `HolderState` (stamped by `request_deposit` and `claim_shares`) ships in the pilot, so every wallet has a stamp when phase 2 is enabled. Stamping at the request stops a holder of old shares from exiting them while a new deposit sits fulfilled but unclaimed.
8. **Upgrade readiness.**
   - Layout rules R1–R8 (spec §14.2): `version` and `bump` first, fixed-size types only, "zero means off", append-only fields, in-place updates, pinned sizes.
   - Padding: `VaultConfig` 512 bytes, `VaultState` 256, and 64 for every other account.
   - Real fields from the pilot: `feature_flags`, `mutav_capital_wallet`, `ExitParams` (all zero), `buffer_earmark`, the partial-fill fields and `HolderState`.
   - Phase-2 counters (`InstantExitState`, 88 bytes) are carved from `VaultState._reserved`. Phase-2 seed prefixes are reserved now.
   - Layout freeze before the first deploy: statuses as `u8` constants, `_reserved` tails on `Caps` / `PriceParams` / `ExitParams`, `MAX_ADAPTERS` pinned, `ConfigUpdated` in its final form (`field: u16, old/new: [u8; 32]`), and a version guard (`account.version ≤ PROGRAM_LAYOUT_VERSION`, else `UnsupportedVersion`).
9. **Two-step enable.** The pilot's `set_config` rejects any feature bit it does not support. Enabling takes (a) a Squads-timelocked, verified program upgrade, then (b) a separate timelocked `set_config` that sets the bit. Existing instructions, events and errors never change (additive IDL, CI `idl-compat` check). Rollback is a pause plus clearing the flag, or a timelocked downgrade (pause → clear flag → `buffer_earmark == 0` → downgrade) that the padding rules make safe. Each upgrade approval checks the multisig's `config_authority` is unset and decodes every instruction in the vault transaction.

## Consequences

- **Phase 2 changes no solvency formula and no claim code.** The pilot audits the earmark-aware maths with the earmark at 0 and injected in tests, and the `pay_claim` diff at upgrade time must be empty.
- **A deliberate exception to ADR 0008.** Instant exits are neither FIFO nor admin-fulfilled. The protection against investors timing their exits moves from "admin-fulfilled FIFO" alone to the notice gate, the holding period, the caps and the headroom switch-off. A notice flagged late is the main residual risk, and it is operational.
- **The pilot queue also waits on notices.** This closes insider timing through admin-timed fills in the pilot, at the cost of queue liveness while notices are open (MUTAV can always reopen by provisioning the leg fully). The pilot gains two operator instructions, the `ClaimNotice` account and three errors (`ClaimNoticePending`, `NoticeNotResolved`, `UnsupportedVersion`).
- **ADR 0009 amended.** The capital wallet is no longer "unknown to config". It is disclosed on-chain, which also serves PC-34.
- **Bars are wallet-level.** Freely transferable shares (PC-32) weaken them, as well as the holding period and the per-wallet caps. Identity-keyed caps or transfer limits stay open (spec §12 Q30).
- **Slightly larger accounts and IDL in the pilot,** for a few hundredths of a SOL in rent. The pilot also gains `FeatureNotSupported`, new events and stricter liquidity rules for fills and allocations.
- **Operational load.** Phase 2 needs a buffer crank, a claim-notice discipline in mutav-app, and an upgrade runbook (spec §14.5) with verified builds and bundled Squads proposals.
- **Open:** the haircut parameters, the earmark form, one multisig or two (the Squads time lock covers every admin action, including `fulfil_redeems`), and the notice SLA. See spec §12 Q15 and Q24–Q31.
