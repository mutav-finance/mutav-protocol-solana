# Demo: Colosseum product demo

Material for MUTAV's ≤3-minute product demo video for the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair.

**Status:** the voiceover is v10 and frozen (232 words, about 1:38 of speech in the 2:19.5 cut; [`production/vo-script.md`](production/vo-script.md)). Narration: AI voice by ElevenLabs (stock voice "Eric"). The pro cut is in production; its export brings the final captions. v10 is a person talking ("we", "let's"): the cold open puts MUTAV on the agency's side (the agency requires a guarantee; other solutions take up to sixty days), chapter 2 lands "Filed to paid, on-chain: sixty-five seconds. Not sixty days.", chapter 3 names no fund or shares, and the close reads "Check it on Solana". The chapter 3 finale card reads "R$100,000 in → R$115,169 out" with "Simulation of reserve mechanics · not an expected return".

The cut is the v9 hybrid structure (2:19.5; scale framing: the devnet amounts are sized to the test tokens available, a caption says so, the devnet captions lead with ratios, chapter 3 runs at the pilot plan's scale and the close names the pilot plan's R$1M reserve, tagged "plan"): an agency beat (the agency dashboard, 19 agencies with letters of intent), then three chapters, one per pitch pillar: 1 · programmable, transparent reserve (Ana's lease on-chain, the next one refused, and the refusal itself landing on-chain as a failed transaction); 2 · programmable payments, settled in minutes (the claim runs live against an elapsed timer, then the program's safeguards that let the agency platform drive these steps next); 3 · capital efficiency (a ledger-driven motion piece from a local simulation on an accelerated clock: six months of guarantee fees and the reserve's yield in, at the pilot plan's scale, growing to a million-real reserve; small claims out, a capital provider's R$100,000 in → R$115,169 out, labelled "Simulation of reserve mechanics · not an expected return"). Chapters 1–2 run live on devnet, with the transactions shown on Solana Explorer, a receipt after each and a running transaction rail; the close points to `/reserve/verify`. The capture harness on `feat/demo-capture` is outdated (pre-#45 routes): capture by hand or update it; [`app-requirements.md`](app-requirements.md) lists what it and the app must change for v8–v9, and what is pending the app team.

Honesty labels used throughout: the devnet amounts are sized to the test tokens available (the scale caption; large figures come only from the pilot plan, tagged "plan", or the local simulation), the agency platform predates the hackathon (the agency beat is a local copy with fictional data, and its connection to the operator is roadmap; the safeguards it would run inside are on-chain today), the PIX transfer to the landlord is simulated on devnet (only its settlement record is on-chain), chapters 1–2 run on devnet, and chapter 3 is a local simulation with a mock BRS mint, not an expected return. Ana, the agency names and the lease references are fictional.
