MUTAV · Colosseum Crypto World's Fair · Product demo storyboard · v3

One lease, refused and repaid.

Ana's lease goes on Solana. The next one is refused by the coverage gate and nothing moves. Her missed rent is claimed, paid and settled while the reserve stays covered. Then the same reserve pays the capital that funds it: R$4,000 in, R$4,057.45 out.

Runtime2:45
Voiceover294 words
Pace~140–150 wpm
DeadlineOct 12 · 23:59 BRT

Centrepiece · 0:52

The refusal

Ana's lease fits. The next equal lease is R$1,000 short of required coverage; sent anyway, the program refuses it with InsufficientFreeCapital. The meter does not move.

Payoff · 2:02

Fees in, capital out

After the claim, the book's guarantee fees lift NAV per share above the deposit price, and a withdrawal is filled from free capital: R$4,000 in, R$4,057.45 out. In reais; never a rate.

Recorded on Solana devnet at reserve.mutav.finance · numbers checked against ADR 0028 (c = 0.25) · Ana, agency names and lease references are fictional · Companion: script.md

The staged state

Numbers that all hold at once

Plan B, the chosen set. ADR 0028 values: c = 0.25 (required = max(⌈0.25 × remaining cover⌉, provisions)), max_tvl R$1M, R$120k per guarantee, claim caps R$15k per call and R$20k per 31 days, requests R$1k–R$100k, fee take 20%. Shares are minted 1:1 at the first fills (virtual offset 1), so NAV per share = net assets ÷ 44,000. Total devnet BRS needed ≈ R$46,040 (≈ R$46,396 with the optional sweep).

What cannot be true with a realistic single month. At a 12% guarantee fee and a 20% take, one month of fees beats a one-month-rent claim only if the book has more than 10.4 leases (0.8 × 0.12 × N > 1). With the BRS known to be in hand (≈ R$50k) the book holds at most 9 leases at c = 0.25. So the payoff uses two months of the book's fees in one step, captioned as such, rather than an inflated fee or a token claim. The optional issuer-revenue sweep adds a simulated month on top.
