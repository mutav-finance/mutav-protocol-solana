Three pillars, one lease.

A hybrid cut. The cold open and chapters 1–2 run live on Solana devnet (reserve.mutav.finance), with a short agency beat (a browser mockup of the agency dashboard from a local copy with fictional data): Ana's lease goes on-chain, the next one is refused and the refusal itself lands on-chain, her missed rent is claimed and settled, and the chapter closes on the on-chain safeguards that let the agency platform drive these steps next. Every devnet transaction is shown: short Explorer flashes, a receipt after each action and a running transaction rail. Chapter 3 is a ledger-driven motion piece from a local simulation on an accelerated clock, at the pilot plan's scale: six months growing to a million-real reserve, guarantee fees and the reserve's yield in, small claims out, and a capital provider's R$100,000 in → R$115,169 out. The close points to /reserve/verify and names the pilot plan. The devnet amounts are sized to the test tokens available: a scale caption says so, the devnet captions lead with ratios (every real backs four in cover), and the large numbers come only from the pilot plan (tagged "plan") or the local simulation (labelled).
