MUTAV · Colosseum Crypto World's Fair · Product demo storyboard · v5

Three pillars, one lease.

The demo follows Ana's lease through MUTAV's three pitch pillars: 1 · a programmable, transparent reserve (her lease goes on-chain, the next one is refused); 2 · settled in minutes (her missed rent, claimed live against a timer); 3 · capital efficiency (shown as end states: capacity, fees, share value, capital out above what went in).

Say it like this: "CPF" as letters; "PIX" as "peeks"; "seguro-fiança" in Portuguese; "real" (the currency) as "ray-AL"; the agency line (12 words) fits its 5 s flash at 150 wpm.
Production note · [N]: [N] comes from the real on-chain timestamps (settled_at − filed_at on the ClaimFiling PDA). The unit is "seconds" if it is under 2 minutes and "minutes" otherwise; round down, never up. The line is about this on-chain run: it does not claim that real-world claims settle in seconds, since the operator checks each claim first (the pitch covers that).
