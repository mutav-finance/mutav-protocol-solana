A hybrid cut. The cold open, the agency flash and chapters 1–2 run live on Solana devnet (reserve.mutav.finance): Ana's lease goes on-chain, the next one is refused, her missed rent is claimed and settled in one take. 2 · Capture

  • Capture harness: Chromium's screencast delivers 1080p at 30 fps; 2× stills are saved on holds only. Zooms over motion ≤ 1.3×; zooms on holds up to 2×, from the stills.
  • Agency records: Before capture, load the operator's agency records into the recording browser.
  • Wallet: the injected disposable test wallet signs; no pop-ups. Signatures show as "Signed by …" chips.
  • Five live clicks on camera: register, the refused send, file, pay, settle. Log each transaction's on-chain time; the timer overlay is driven by them.
  • End states: the Explorer shot (F4) is captured on devnet after F3; chapter 3 (F10–F13) is captured on localnet after the six simulated months have run.

3 · Voice, captions, edit, export

  1. Rough cut the clips to the frame timings with the overlays.
  2. Record the VO to the cut: quiet soft room, mic 10–15 cm with a pop filter, peaks around −12 dBFS, 140–150 wpm, two or three takes per line, 5 s of room tone. Fill in [N] first.
  3. Captions: English VO with English burned-in captions (under 60 characters, with the specific numbers) plus an English SRT. Optional: a PT-BR SRT track for the Superteam Brazil jury.
  4. Fine-tune so each on-screen event lands on its words. Music none or very low, out under the F7 silence.
  5. Export ≤ 2:15, H.264 1080p30, AAC 48 kHz, about −14 LUFS. Upload unlisted (YouTube preferred, or Loom); check the field on the Colosseum submission form. Put the SUSEP Circ. 621/2021 art. 43 source in the description.
