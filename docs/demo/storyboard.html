One lease, end to end.
The chain is the proof.

Ana needs a guarantor to rent in Passo Fundo. Her agency uses MUTAV. Her lease goes on Solana, a second lease is refused by the coverage gate, her missed rent is claimed, paid and settled, and anyone can check every step.

The refusal

One more lease, the same size. The preview turns red. We send it anyway, and the program refuses it with InsufficientFreeCapital. MUTAV can't sign a guarantee the reserve can't back.

Why the restore works (checked in programs/mutav, branch feat/interfaces): fulfil_deposits is allowed while under-covered. It is "the recapitalization path (ADR 0008)" (capital/fulfil_deposits.rs:2-3), and its only gates are pause and the fulfil halt (:75-77), plus the TVL cap (:168-172). request_deposit checks only pause (capital/request_deposit.rs:76). Mode changes only in refresh (public/refresh.rs:105-110); pay_claim has no mode check (operator/pay_claim.rs:3,157). So after the restore, someone must send refresh or the badge keeps the last mode. Alternative without Squads: close_guarantee RELEASED on two R$18k demo leases (not gated, not paused: operator/close_guarantee.rs:1-2), giving required R$5,700 against R$7,960, then refresh. It shrinks the book to 4 active leases, so the deposit is preferred.
The app can't do these on devnet: the stress-buffer term (in config, read by no rule in the PR #37 binary); an Explorer link for the refusal (it fails in pre-send simulation); claims above R$10k (they need approve_claim, Wave 2); a term or end date on the Guarantee account (the program stores cover legs and registered_at only, so the VO doesn't mention a term); real issuer income (Nora doesn't pay on devnet); a real PIX transfer (simulated, and only its record is on-chain).

2 · Recording setup

  • Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps. Record the browser window at exactly 1920×1080 (no full-screen menu bar), or crop to it.
  • Browser: a fresh Chrome profile: only Phantom installed, bookmarks bar hidden (⌘⇧B), no other extensions, default theme. Zoom 125%; check that the /operator form fits without horizontal scroll.
  • Phantom: network devnet, operator account selected, auto-lock off for the session, the testnet-mode banner understood.
  • Mac: Focus/Do Not Disturb on, Slack and Mail closed, the Dock hidden, a clean desktop.
  • Tabs pre-opened in order: /operator#console · /reserve#coverage · /reserve#claims · /reserve#health · the Explorer tab for guarantee #1 (warm).
  • Recorder: Screen Studio, or OBS (Display Capture, 1080p, CRF ~18). Turn on a cursor highlight if the tool has it, and keep the cursor still between clicks.
  • One screen clip per beat: F2–F5, F6–F7, F8–F10, F11–F12. Nothing has to be continuous: let confirmations run, and they are cut in the edit. Name files like B3-F06-take2.mov.
  • RPC check: /api/ledger answers in under 2 s right before each clip.

3 · Order of work: picture first, then voice

  1. Capture the screen clips beat by beat, silently. Retake any clip freely: see each frame's Retake note for what a redo consumes on-chain.
  2. Rough cut the picture to the frame timings: cut every wait and keep each beat near its slot.

  • Captions on: burn in open captions, since judges often watch muted, and also upload an .srt.
  • Cut every wait: Phantom pop-ups (keep at most 1 s), "Confirming…", and page loads.
  • Zoom-ins at 150–200% with a 300 ms ease. Use amber outline highlights matching the brand (#E8A020) and keep everything else neutral.
  • Music: none, or very low ambient (about −30 dB under the VO), dipped out at the two climaxes.
  • Lower thirds in the brand style: mono, small, with an amber tick. Use hard cuts only, no transitions.
