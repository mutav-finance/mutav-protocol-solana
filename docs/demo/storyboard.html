MUTAV · Colosseum Crypto World's Fair · Product demo storyboard · v4

Ana needs a guarantor.
The chain is the proof.

Ana's lease goes on Solana. One more lease is refused by the coverage gate, and nothing moves. Her missed rent is claimed, paid and settled while the reserve stays covered. Even after her claim, each share is worth more than before, and a capital provider takes out more than they put in.

The refusal

"So what if we try to sign one more lease?" It needs R$45,000; the reserve holds R$44,000. Click: refused, nothing moves, three seconds of silence.

Plan B, with a 6× + 6× cover so the register form needs no change (rent R$1,500, cover R$18,000). ADR 0028: c = 0.25 (required = max(⌈0.25 × remaining cover⌉, provisions)), max_tvl R$1M, R$120k per guarantee, claim caps R$15k per call and R$20k per 31 days, requests R$1k–R$100k, fee take 20%. Narration

Full script · 235 words

Verbatim. About 1:35–1:40 of speech at 140–150 wpm inside 2:45; the rest is holds, the F7 silence and the close. Spoken amounts in reais: three. [N] is filled in after capture.

0:00Ana wants to rent an apartment in Passo Fundo. Her landlord wants a guarantor. MUTAV is that guarantor, and every real it promises is backed on Solana.

0:11Her agency works in MUTAV's platform. Every lease it signs becomes an account on Solana.

0:18Our operator puts the lease on-chain: eighteen thousand reais of cover, one account per lease, with an opaque ID. No name, no CPF.

0:37The public reserve updates at once. It holds BRS, Nora's Brazilian real stablecoin, and must keep a quarter of everything it guarantees.

0:48So what if we try to sign one more lease? It needs forty-five thousand. The reserve holds forty-four. [click] The program refuses. Nothing moves. MUTAV can't promise what its reserve can't back. [3 s silence]

1:12Now say Ana misses a month. The claim is filed, the money is set aside, and the reserve pays MUTAV's payments account, within a cap set by the admin multisig. The PIX to the agency is recorded on-chain: filed to settled in [N] minutes. Seguro-fiança allows up to thirty days.

1:50Meanwhile, guarantee fees from every lease flow into the reserve. Even after paying Ana's claim, each share is worth more than before. A capital provider takes out more than they put in, only from capital above what the guarantees require, and only after claims. The same reserve that protects the landlord pays the people who fund it.

2:30MUTAV. Brazil's onchain rent guarantee. Open source, live on devnet.

Say it like this: "CPF" as letters; "PIX" as "peeks"; "seguro-fiança" in Portuguese; "real" (the currency) as "ray-AL".
